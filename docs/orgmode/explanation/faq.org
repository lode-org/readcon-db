** Why is this a separate crate?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: why-is-this-a-separate-crate
:END:
[[https://lode-org.github.io/readcon-core/][readcon-core]] is the decoder and writer for one stream or file. *readcon-db* owns the LMDB tree, secondary indexes, and multi-reader mmap. Once structures are CON text, the same files ingest here without a second structure dialect.

** What is authoritative?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-is-authoritative
:END:
UTF-8 CON text. Hashes, dedup, join/split, and =reindex= run on the stored blobs. Cooked SoA / RCSO / H5MD arrays are derived and discardable.

** Is this SQL?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: is-this-sql
:END:
No. =Select= is an explicit builder (symbol, (N), energy, flags, exact hash) over B-tree postings. There is no planner.

** Where do large campaigns go?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: where-do-large-campaigns-go
:END:
CON text stays the structure contract. This crate indexes it. Multi-frame CON files and =iter_con= in core cover trajectory-style loads. For many writers, use @@rst::doc:`/howto/campaign`@@ (one writer per shard).

** How do XYZ, PDB, GRO fit in?
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: how-do-xyz-pdb-gro-fit-in
:END:
They are ingress in *readcon-core* (chemfiles). Convert to CON, then ingest. Optional =to_ase= is calculator hand-off only; ASE is not on the read path.
